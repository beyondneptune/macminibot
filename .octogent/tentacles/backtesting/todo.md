- [ ] **11. False-discovery floor (FDF) at promotion** — Shuffle each feature column independently (per-column permutation preserves marginals, destroys cross-sectional signal), refit the strategy, record DSR. Repeat 100×. Promotion DSR must exceed the 95th-pct of the null distribution. Run once per promotion candidate, not per backtest. Cost: ~100× inner-loop refit; acceptable at promotion cadence. Closes the "DSR > 0.95 but on noise that happened to fit" hole that DSR's multiple-trial correction alone doesn't catch.

- [ ] **12. Multi-strategy family-wise gate (Romano-Wolf stepdown)** — Once >1 distinct strategy class is in the candidate pool (momentum, mean-rev, vol-carry, etc.), DSR's per-strategy multiple-trial correction is insufficient: comparing the best of K strategy families against the null requires controlling FWER across families. Implement Romano-Wolf stepdown (Econometrica 2005) over the bootstrap distribution of OOS Sharpes; expose `family_adjusted_pvalue` per candidate; **promotion requires family-adjusted p < 0.05** in addition to DSR ≥ 0.95 and FDF. Cross-check against White's Reality Check (Econometrica 2000) and Hansen's SPA (J. Bus. Econ. Stat. 2005) on a fixture so the chosen test isn't a one-off. Not on v1 critical path (single strategy class today); land **before** the second strategy family is proposed for promotion, otherwise p-hacking-by-strategy-shopping is undetected.
